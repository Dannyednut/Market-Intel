### [W1b-2] OCC GENIUS Act final rule — PUBLISHED; kill trigger #1 DISCHARGED (Grade A)

**The OCC finalized its GENIUS Act payment-stablecoin rule on October 21, 2026; Federal Register publication October 23, 2026 (Vol. 91); effective January 18, 2027** — the same day the GENIUS Act itself takes effect (multiple sources: stablecoininsider.org primary readout Oct 23; Mindplex Oct 21; Binance Square Oct 25; X/Tokenized Finance Alert Oct 25; FR Vol. 91 No. 204 info-collection notice Oct 23). The calendar's Oct 31 kill trigger #1 ("no final rule text by Oct 31") **never fires — the text landed 8–10 days inside the window**, and the effective date lands exactly on the calendar's Jan 18, 2027 fallback horizon.

Substance — the **GENIUS distribution-scope decision and distributor-yield interpretive path are now decided** (the two calendar-critical questions):

- **Yield ban retained but narrowed in scope**: issuers cannot pay holders interest/yield "solely" for holding, using, or retaining the stablecoin. **No grandfathering** for pre-GENIUS reward programs, no de minimis exemption, no safe harbor for white-label partners or arrangements that are securities. Transaction-based payments that are not yield or not paid solely for holding/use/retention are outside the ban.
- **The NPRM's rebuttable presumption against third-party rewards was DROPPED** — affiliate and related third-party reward arrangements are no longer auto-deemed prohibited yield. This **opens the distributor-yield interpretive path** for independent (non-affiliate) distributor rewards — the most decision-relevant change for exchange/distribution economics. Issuer-affiliate-funded yield remains inside the ban perimeter.
- **Reserve/liquidity easing**: daily liquidity requirement cut 10% → 5% of reserves; weekly liquidity and weighted-average-maturity requirements dropped; concentration limit re-keyed per-bank → per-counterparty 40% (USTs, appropriate custodians, centrally cleared repo excluded).
- **Redemption mechanics softened**: the automatic 7-day redemption extension was removed; a >10%/24h redemption spike now triggers OCC notification within 24 hours, with extensions at OCC discretion. Standard clock: 2 business days.
- **Capital unchanged and hardened in duration**: $5M floor for all issuers (extended beyond the de novo period) + 12-months-of-expenses operational backstop (up to 50% qualifying debt).
- **Application infrastructure not yet live**: applications gated on OMB approval of the new form collection (comments due **Nov 23, 2026**); OCC will announce acceptance by press release. AML/BSA/sanctions rules follow separately with Treasury. Per Binance Square readout: "The OCC has not yet announced when it will begin accepting" applications.

Background context (different rulemakings, temperature only): ICBA's APA suit against OCC (filed Oct 2, Mar 2 trust-bank rule + IL 1176) and the second community-bank suit (Oct 9) remain pending — litigation pressure on OCC chartering continues, but does not touch the GENIUS final rule's effective date.
