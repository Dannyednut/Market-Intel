## D. RESEARCH SEEDS (carried into Discovery)

1. Stablecoin off-ramps are the new African moat (Circle/Tazapay, Kora One Rail, WalaPay convergence in one month) — who owns compliant local-currency off-ramps into mobile money wins; on-ramp tech commoditizing.
2. Regulator-imposed free instant payments (PI-SPI, SA NPS opening, CBN rules, BSP cap) are systematically destroying P2P/agent fee pools — transfer-margin businesses must migrate to credit or acquiring within ~18 months.
3. African fintech exits repricing through local public markets (NYSE/EGX/HKEX), not Western acquirers — "local-listing arbitrage" re-rating pan-African lenders ahead of stale VC marks.
4. Venue layer consolidating into TradFi-crypto super-venues (ICE-OKX, Nasdaq-Payward, LSE-Kraken) — whitespace for neutral cross-rail clearing/interop between tokenized and conventional equities.
5. Regulatory arbitrage is the key market-share variable — Binance squeezed out of EU as US opens; advantage to licensed locals (359 CASPs, banks ~23%) and UAE staging entities.
6. Spot fee compression + fading retail volumes push exchanges toward derivatives-and-infrastructure P&L — perps on everything + B2B stablecoin rails is the next battleground.
7. Yield-routing is the new moat — pair a non-yielding stablecoin with an affiliated tokenized MMF (Circle USYC playbook, 21-bank consortium copying); whitespace: native sweep of idle stablecoin balances into tokenized Treasury funds.
8. Distribution war of 2027 — bank-consortium USD token launches into a shrinking stablecoin pool with USDT locked out of EU; edge sits with EM on/off-ramp controllers (Nigeria: CBN cut 350bp AND embraced stablecoins; ~60% of SSA flow).
9. Market infrastructure is swallowing fintech — ICE-MarketAxess, $22B+ Kalshi, Stripe-PayPal approach, MetaMask payments pivot; next targets: custody/tokenization rails enabling regulated on-chain yield.
