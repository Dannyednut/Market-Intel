## 5. NEW STRUCTURAL PROBLEMS

Two Cycle 002 mechanisms are not covered by P1-P7 and clear the evidence bar. A third candidate is declined.

**P8. Issuer-content veto: issuers are the gatekeepers of the tokenized 24/7 equity market.**
Refs: [D1][W6][E1]
MECHANISM: The Innovation Exemption embeds issuer-objection rights over tokenized trading of listed shares. Within two weeks of OKXICE's Oct 5 filing, nine+ mega-cap issuers (Microsoft, Alphabet, Goldman, Broadcom, Verizon, UnitedHealth, CoreWeave, Qualcomm, Caterpillar, McDonald's, Western Digital) exercised them [D1]; the competing Nasdaq gateway is explicitly "issuer-first" [W6]. Venue technology (AMMs, permissioned chains) is no longer the scarce input — **issuer consent is**. That concentrates gatekeeping power in the largest issuers, fragments the 24/7 universe (objectors' mega-caps absent from tokenized venues), and creates a new bargaining layer over venue fees, collateral treatment, and after-hours price formation.
EXPOSED: crypto-native tokenized venues (OKXICE launch viability); liquidity providers needing full-universe access; the 24/7-equity demand thesis itself; small venues without issuer relationships.
BENEFICIARIES: incumbent exchange groups with issuer relationships (Nasdaq, LSE, ICE); issuers themselves (consent becomes a licensing asset they can monetize or blockade); extended-hours incumbent venues that inherit the 24/7 mandate if tokenization stalls.
SEVERITY: **Medium-high** for the tokenized-equity trajectory (delays P6's equities leg 2-4 quarters or reroutes it through incumbents); medium market-wide.
TRAJECTORY (6-12mo): either issuers **monetize** consent (a venue-issuer licensing market emerges — a new toll on equity trading) or they **blockade** (tokenized venues launch mid-cap-only, liquidity fragments, 24/7 arrives via incumbent extended hours instead). Resolution = marker 4.

**P9. Last-mile rail capture: EM off-ramp spreads are revocable licences granted by the party being disintermediated.**
Refs: [2-b][D6][D10][2-a#6][M2]
MECHANISM: The verified EM off-ramp margin stack (net 40-280bps; retail FX spread 150-350bps; plus the under-modeled local-currency float leg — NGN float at 16.84%) exists only with the **payout-rail owner's permission**. The Partna×M-Pesa KES corridor — paused May 7 2026 with **no alternative live route** — is the first eviction [2-b]. Rail owners are simultaneously building their own stablecoin corridors (Visa+M-PESA+Onafriq DRC pilot) or joining bank consortia (Standard Bank, Absa); licence-passporting (Kenya-Rwanda MoU) erodes the rent layer that justified exclusivity; and a third vector compounds both: **regulator de-platforming** (BSP restricting Coins.ph inbound rails [2-a#6]). Circle's 40-market push depends on exactly these local banking partners [D6]. The disintermediation margin lives at the mercy of the disintermediated.
EXPOSED: stablecoin off-ramp operators (Partna, Kora, Yellow Card, Bitnob); EM yield routers whose economics clear only via the FX leg [2-c]; inbound-flow franchises on single payout legs; CBN data-localisation (Jan 2027, no extension [M2]) raises the cost floor of every Nigeria-dependent leg.
BENEFICIARIES: MNOs (Safaricom-class); payout-monopoly banks (Standard Bank, Absa); card networks selling internalized alternatives (Visa×Mesh, 175M merchants [D10]); incumbent MTOs with rail-independent cash networks.
SEVERITY: **High** for the EM off-ramp/rails business model — converts the deep-dive's 60-100bps "moat" into a revocable licence with single-point failure.
TRAJECTORY (12-24mo): corridor evictions and JV-consolidation into rail owners; spreads compress fastest where FX liberalizes (Kenya's 0-1.7% premium is the end-state preview); durable EM margin migrates to owned payout legs, local-currency float yield, and compliance wraps. Resolution = marker 8.

**Declined: "float vs flow divergence" as a standalone problem.** The evidence [D3] is strong, but the mechanism, exposed parties, and beneficiaries are P1's — formalizing separately would double-count. Absorbed into P1's reframe (two-speed category), tracked as marker 3. The CMC/rwa.xyz $315B-vs-$300.9B gap is a measurement item for C003, not a market-structure problem.
