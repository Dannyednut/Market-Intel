## CARD 1 — Dual-Rail Merchant Network → **VERDICT: REVISED** (spread confirmed; moat relocated to the payout leg)

**Audit.** The core spread assumption survives its first hard test and comes back **stronger than assumed**: B2B/API off-ramp realizes **80–150bps gross / 45–115bps net** (Nigeria), retail 200–270bps gross, Ghana 150–350bps — "60–100bps was a conservative price point, not a ceiling" [2-b]. dLocal's blended **2.68%** take on $40.8B TPV [2-b:F4] and Tazapay's published **0.8–2.5%** [2-b:F5] confirm the wholesale price ladder. The free-rail collection leg holds (NIP wholesale ₦2/txn; free ≤₦5,000 [2-b:F3]) — with Kenya the exception (M-Pesa till collect up to 0.55%). But three new constraints reshape the card:

1. **The spread is an official-parallel policy artifact.** Live USDT premiums: Nigeria **+2.2–2.7%**, Ghana **+3.55%** (widest), Kenya **0–1.7%** [2-b:F6]. USDT/NGN sits between official ₦1,333.5 and parallel ₦1,375–1,390 — the premium *is* the FX-control wedge in disguise. Kenya is the end-state preview: liberalized FX = thin premium = thin margin. Corridor selection must therefore weight **premium duration**, not premium level.
2. **Last-mile de-platforming is real and precedented.** Partna's KES M-Pesa corridor has been paused since 2026-05-07 with **no alternative live route** [2-b:F7]. Without an owned or contractually exclusive payout leg, the spread is a revocable license granted by an MNO/bank incentivized to internalize it (Visa×M-PESA×Onafriq DRC pilot [2-b:F8]; WU×dLocal building proprietary payout [F12]). **An exclusive payout leg is now a REQUIRED component** — formalized as new Card 7 below.
3. **Card networks press the merchant FX edge from above.** Visa×Mesh makes stablecoin balances spendable at **175M+ merchant locations** [D10]; stablecoin payment volume grew **+45% to ~$482B** YTD with card/stripe rails as the driver [D3]. Card-rail spend commoditizes the merchant acceptance leg; what it cannot commoditize is cross-border settlement FX and payout exclusivity — which is where the card now lives. Mitigation: **Mastercard Stablecoin Security screening as compliance partner** [D10] — converts the observed screening/mule cost lines (2–5bps + 5–15bps [2-b]) into an integrated feature and a regulator-facing asset.

Also upgraded: **NGN float yield as a second margin pool** — 364-day T-bill stop at **16.84%** (Oct 14) [2-b:F9] ⇒ ~4.6bps/day; a 10-day prefunding cycle adds **~46bps on top of FX spread** per cycle (eng. illustration: $50M/mo throughput × 10-day cycle ≈ $17M average float ⇒ ~$2.8M/yr float income vs ~$4.8M/yr at 80bps FX — float ≈ 37% additive). And the binding regulatory risk is **reframed from transfer-fee caps to FX-spread disclosure rules** — the BSP went past caps to *free* P2P (Aug 15, 2026 [2-b:F1]), and the IMF publicly urged Nigeria on stablecoin cross-border flows (6/16/26 [2-b]); a "official-rate + small fee" disclosure regime would legislate away pool #1.

## CARD 5 — Agent-Network Reversal → **VERDICT: KILLED** (both premises falsified by the Baxi outcome; salvage into Card 7)

**Kill reasoning.** Cycle 001's construct rested on two premises; both failed their first live test:
1. **Acquisition premise falsified.** Onafriq's Baxi wind-down did **not** create a distress market — agents were **transitioned to another principal bank for free** [2-b:F12]. The clearing price for wind-down agent estates is **zero**, and the zero-price buyer is a *bank* (CBN's single-principal rule structurally favors banks as receiving principals). There is no 0.5–1.5x replacement-cost entry for a non-bank upstart: the scarce asset was never the network — it is the **principal licence**, and networks attach to it for free.
2. **Asset premise falsified.** A free transition proves the agent relationship is **portable at zero switching cost**. "You cannot download an agent" was true but irrelevant — agents multi-home and principals churn them at no cost, so Cycle 001's retention-based moat fails its first observation. (Cycle 001's own kill trigger — retention <60% at 6 months — would almost certainly fire in a world of free transitions.)
3. **Demand premise eroding.** The cash-handling margin pool is being regulated toward zero on the same template as transfer fees: SA's cash utility with free small withdrawals and shared ATMs (pilot Jan 2027) [D8], BSP free P2P [2-b:F1], PI-SPI free [F8].
4. **Margin premise migrates.** The 30–60bps off-ramp commissions the card targeted are better captured as a **wholesale payout toll on leased capacity** (Card 7) — without owning agent estates, float insurance, or the compliance exposure that killed Baxi [F11].
- **Salvage**: float-velocity unit logic and exchange/B2B offtake economics fold into **Card 7** as revenue lines; the agent-estate option survives only inside a licensed-bank JV — **monitor, don't fund**. Re-opening condition (deliberately narrow): a future wind-down where a *non-bank* receives a free or nominal transition, proving third-party portability — else the kill stands.

- **Construct**: An issuer-side **licensing/consent agent**. WHO does WHAT: the desk signs issuers to standardized tokenized-listing consent agreements (tiered scope: trading-only / trading + corporate-actions data license / brand + venue-list controls; fee schedules per tier); packages consented symbols into venue-licensable bundles; runs objection monitoring and consent administration.
