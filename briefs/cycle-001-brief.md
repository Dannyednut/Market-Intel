## Key judgments

1. The stablecoin contraction is **rate-driven, not risk-off**: Tether shrank while RLUSD grew +50% m/m — float is being routed, not abandoned.
2. Free A2A rails convert payments volume into a public good within ~18 months per market; margin migrates to credit, merchant acquiring, and cross-border FX.
3. African fintech capital is flipping from venture equity to bank balance sheets (Absa–Lendable $255M credit fund; OPay NYSE filing; MNT-Halan 11x oversubscribed).
4. Two hard clocks: **SA non-bank payments opening mid-2027** and the **21-bank consortium stablecoin in H1 2027**.
